Question 4 of 20

Which principle states that it is impossible to simultaneously know both the precise position and momentum of a particle?

auto_awesome

AI Explanation

The Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle is a fundamental concept in quantum mechanics, formulated by Werner Heisenberg in 1927. It implies that the more precisely the position of some particle is determined, the less precisely its momentum can be known from initial conditions, and vice versa. This is not a limitation of measuring instruments, but a fundamental property of quantum systems.
